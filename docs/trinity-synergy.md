# PLANCK architecture for VRIK HIGGS and PLANCK synergy

The synergy gives Skyrim VR mods a shared foundation with clear owners. HIGGS owns hand states, input and physical hand use. VRIK supplies the body and virtual-hand pose. PLANCK owns live NPC physics, ragdoll lifetime and its physical-hit processing. PPB supplies anatomical surfaces/fitting and touch evidence. Body Slots owns all slot mechanics; consumers retain their gameplay and content rules.

**Body Slots** owns creation, configuration, body/equipment binding, geometry, detection, activation and presentation. [Morphbench](https://github.com/Vhodnoylogin/morphbench) supplies offline authoring. VRIK provides pose observations rather than a new-slot execution path. Native14 compatibility is separate. Actual implementation and remaining gaps are distinguished in the [implementation ledger](https://github.com/Vhodnoylogin/body-pouches/blob/main/docs/trinity-synergy/implementation-status.md).

The only maintained source of the full concept and illustrative pseudo-ABI is [the English concept in Body Pouches](https://github.com/Vhodnoylogin/body-pouches/blob/main/docs/trinity-synergy/concept-en.md), with [a Russian counterpart](https://github.com/Vhodnoylogin/body-pouches/blob/main/docs/trinity-synergy/concept-ru.md). This file records PLANCK-specific requirements, not another copy of the concept. Body Pouches intends to adopt the architecture; it is not an available SDK or an agreement with the framework authors.

## Required architectural changes in PLANCK

1. **Expose copied NPC state and validated endpoints.** Provide actor, bone and body metadata, coordinate spaces and generations. Attachment requests return endpoints with a declared lifetime rather than private ragdoll pointers. Ragdoll replacement, actor unload, world replacement and game load invalidate dependent endpoints and constraints.

2. **Separate hit candidates from committed outcomes.** Offer a bounded policy stage before the relevant physical-hit commitment, followed by immutable results. Observing a candidate does not permit changing damage afterward. Define scope, conflict resolution and visible user priorities where alternatives are selectable; process each physical hit once.

3. **Replace global tuning with scoped policies.** Registered consumers request policies for their declared actor, weapon or interaction and owned leases for temporary effects. Weight, parry and penetration consumers keep their gameplay rules instead of changing unrelated weapons' thresholds or restoring another mod's settings.

4. **Use HIGGS for player-hand ownership and physical requests.** Migrate temporary hand restrictions to owned suppression leases; request explicit release when possession must end and await confirmed results. The current `DropObject`/`UpdateHiggsDrop` path uses legacy `DisableHand` to end an established hold and later clears that bit. HIGGS's new temporary restriction intentionally preserves established holds, so replacing this call with an admission restriction alone would not perform the drop. This path needs a correlated release operation before migration. PLANCK drives NPC animation; HIGGS remains the final actuator for its player hand/weapon bodies. A penetration consumer decides wound/extraction rules, PLANCK validates the NPC endpoint and HIGGS applies the weapon constraint.

5. **Keep slot authoring and body-slot interaction outside PLANCK.** Body Slots owns every slot mechanic. Taking an item from a slot uses Body Slots, its content executor and HIGGS; VRIK supplies pose. PLANCK participates when an action interacts with NPC physics or its hit processing. It does not become a slot registry or inventory/equipment executor.

6. **Make lifecycle and safe phases part of the ABI.** Keep interface 001 and its hit-event contract unchanged. Negotiate additive capabilities with copied size-tagged records, request/executor identities, epochs and explicit callback lifetimes. Support removal with confirmed drainage and queue mutations at their legal phase. Slot binding generation, physical possession and NPC endpoint generation are distinct; never serialize live endpoint or lease handles.

The [additive revision 2 implementation](synergy-api.md) supplies copied NPC/body publications, generation-checked endpoints, scoped hit restriction leases, removable callbacks, real precommit melee/clutter policies and safe-phase capsule geometry adoption through independent engine-created shape clones. A separate revision-3 discovery entry returns the version-1 native endpoint bridge for HIGGS, resolving the live body under graph/world ownership. Revision 001 remains unchanged. Production-core and extracted native-adapter fixtures cover these boundaries; native/game qualification is recorded separately in the [build prerequisites](native-build-prerequisites.md).

Separate negotiated tables now add [actor-scoped drive policies](drive-api.md) and [existing compound-capsule fitting](compound-geometry-api.md). Optional HIGGS native hand-part attribution lets PLANCK recognize owned finger bodies in its existing contact/side path. PPB retains fitting/anatomical interpretation and obtains physical application from these providers; an actual PPB adapter and game acceptance are not delivered by these tables alone.

CommonLib is the second synergy level: reuse its current actor, scene, locking and Havok primitives, and move address handling and repeated technical operations into verified library methods. NPC policy, endpoints and hit commitment remain PLANCK runtime services; common engine code does not take over those decisions.

## Development branches and retained work

`main` retains its existing upstream source. `development` merges author upstream `aed524a5e398f04f10f8f95469153362a88c3681` (0.8.2, verified 2 November 2026), including the author's graph/world deadlock repairs, and retains the former public and private research history, architecture requirements and [bounded lifecycle repairs](../tests/README.md). Reset clears temporary movement/look-at policy under locks, nested synchronous hit dispatch restores the outer event context, and the reset-only deferred cleanup fence remains in place. The new [revision 2 source](synergy-api.md) adds the bounded services described above. Standalone checks establish source behavior; the full native plugin remains unbuilt and untested in Skyrim. The obsolete non-fork `planck-dev` repository was removed after verified preservation.

The former `movement` branch is already an ancestor of the upstream source. The separate `float_precision` experiment at `c7a07f36973bb5451903e1dc83d1bbdecef0e8bf` is retained as an ancestor of `development` through a history-only merge. Its code is not enabled: it contains unfinished double-precision pose hooks, dummy settings and NPC-specific file logging. Inspect that historical commit explicitly when revisiting the experiment. All prior public branches and tags also have a verified external bundle; only `main` and `development` remain published branches.
